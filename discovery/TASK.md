# Research brief
Build on the WSP-Gym results (README.md) with new, novel computational experiments. The goal is
scientifically interesting findings about weight-space programming: what makes a network's weights
readable/repairable from text, what the trained model has and has not learned, where the gym's
claims are robust or fragile, and new tasks or measurements the gym makes possible. Findings must be
rigorous: paired comparisons on the frozen splits, bootstrap CIs, controls, and stated pre-registration
of the main comparison before running it. Avoid re-deriving numbers already in the README unless the
point is a new cut of them.
