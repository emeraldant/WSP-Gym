## What the task is
`backdoor-witness`: a model is shown the weights of a small ReLU network (2 hidden layers) as text plus a one-line boolean specification, and must answer with a "witness" (an input where the network's thresholded output differs from the specification) or `{"clean": true}`. Networks come in matched pairs (index 2p = poisoned twin, 2p+1 = clean twin). A forward pass scores the answer. Every frozen network is claimed to carry an exact mixed-integer certificate: clean twins never disagree with the spec, poisoned twins disagree only inside their planted trigger.
